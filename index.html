Ngao Du

Ngao Du records where you go on your iPhone, quietly and in the background, so that you can geotag your photos later on your Mac.

Coming to the App Store

What Ngao Du does

  • Records your route in the background, even when the app is closed, with barometric altitude if you want it.
  • Starts with your camera. Link a camera over Bluetooth and recording begins when it switches on.
  • Browses by time. Pick any range of days on the map and see where you were.
  • Geotags photos on the Mac, in Lightroom Classic, in Immich, or in photo files.
  • Imports and exports GPX, GeoJSON, and other track files, and uploads to your own Dawarich server.

No account, no server

There is no sign-up and no Ngao Du server. Your location history stays on your iPhone and Mac, synced through your own iCloud account or directly over your local network. Ngao Du collects no analytics and no crash reports.
