Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Ngao Du has no account and no server of its own. The route it records is stored on your iPhone and your Mac. We do not receive it, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

What we collect

Nothing.

Ngao Du has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no backend service. Your recorded points, tracks, places, tags, and imports are stored on your devices. None of it is transmitted to the developer of Ngao Du.

Ngao Du contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, and no third-party trackers. We do not know how often you record, where you went, or whether you use the app at all.

Where your location history lives

Every point Ngao Du records is saved in a database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. With each point it keeps the position, its accuracy, the time, and, if you allow it, the air pressure and barometric altitude.

To keep your iPhone and Mac in step, you choose one of two ways to sync:

  • iCloud — your library is synced through the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account. It is stored by Apple, under your account, and covered by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. Which sync method you chose is also shared between your devices through iCloud.
  • Local Network — your iPhone and Mac talk to each other directly over your own network, encrypted with the pairing code you enter. Nothing passes through iCloud or any server.

What leaves your device, and when

Apart from the sync you choose above, these things can send data off your device. Each happens only when you use that specific feature.

Maps and place names

Maps in Ngao Du are drawn by Apple Maps, which downloads map tiles for the area you are looking at. When Ngao Du names a place you stayed, or suggests nearby places, it sends the coordinates of that place to Apple Maps. This is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Dawarich

Only if you connect it, and only on iPhone. Dawarich is a location-history server that you run yourself or that someone runs for you. When connected, Ngao Du uploads your recorded points and visits to the server address you entered, using the API key you gave it. It can also read points back from that server, to import your older history or to avoid sending duplicates; when a day changes in Ngao Du it replaces that day's points it uploaded earlier, and never touches points that came from other apps.

The server address and API key are stored in the Keychain on your iPhone. What happens to your data on that server is up to whoever runs it.

Immich

Only if you connect it. Immich is a photo server that you run yourself or that someone runs for you. When you geotag photos, Ngao Du asks your Immich server for photos taken in the time range you are looking at, shows their thumbnails, and writes back the locations and tags you choose. The server address and API key are stored in your iCloud Keychain, so that connecting on one device connects the other.

Servers you connect are governed by whoever runs them, not by us. If you enter an address that starts with http:// rather than https://, the connection to that server is not encrypted, and your points or API key can be read by anyone on the network in between. Use https:// unless the server is on a network you trust.

Lightroom Classic and photo files (Mac)

On the Mac, Ngao Du can write locations and keywords into photos in Lightroom Classic, through a plug-in that talks to Ngao Du only on your own Mac, or into photo files in folders you choose. Nothing about this leaves your Mac.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Permissions Ngao Du asks for

Ngao Du requests each of these when you turn on the matching feature. You can change or revoke any of them at any time in Settings.

  • Location, Always — to record your route in the background, even when the app is closed, so you can geotag your photos later. This is the core of the app.
  • Motion & Fitness — to store air pressure and barometric altitude with each recorded point.
  • Bluetooth — to notice when a camera you linked switches on, so recording can start while you shoot. Ngao Du only listens for your cameras; it sends them nothing.
  • Local Network — to sync with Ngao Du on your Mac, and to reach your Dawarich or Immich server when it runs on your own network.
  • Notifications — to remind you to change your camera's clock when you arrive in a new time zone, and, if you turn them on, to tell you when a camera connects or recording starts. These are created on your device.

Location and motion data read by Ngao Du are used only to build your own location history. They are never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Locations are sensitive

A location history can reveal where you live, where you work, who you visit, and when you are away. Ngao Du records it continuously while recording is on, so treat what it holds with the same care.

Points stay on your devices unless you choose iCloud sync, connect a server, or export a file yourself. Files you export (GPX, GeoJSON, and so on) are yours to keep or share, and once they leave Ngao Du we have no control over them.

What Ngao Du never does

  • It never sends your location history to us, because there is no server to send it to.
  • It never uploads to a server you did not connect yourself.
  • It never uses your data for advertising, profiling, or training anything.
  • It never sells or shares your data, because it never receives it.

Children

Ngao Du is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data generally apply to data held by a company. We hold none of yours. Your history is on your own devices: to export it, use the export in the app; to delete it, delete it in the app or remove the app. Copies in iCloud are managed through your Apple Account, and copies on a Dawarich or Immich server through that server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since we have no way to contact you, please check back here if it matters to you.
